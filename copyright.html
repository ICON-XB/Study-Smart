Copyright and Rights Complaints — Study-Smart

Version 2026-09-23-draft. This is a draft pending legal review. Text in double square brackets marks information that must be completed or reviewed before release.

Study-Smart keeps each student's material in their own browser. It does not publish, host or share uploaded material with other users, and scans are processed on the device, or by Google only when the student uses their own Gemini key. Most notice-and-takedown situations therefore do not apply. We still respond to rights complaints about the app itself: its text, images, icons, university presets and names.

How to send a complaint

Email iconicindustries0@gmail.com with the subject "Rights complaint" and include:

  1. Your name and contact details.
  2. The work or right you believe is infringed (for example a logo, trademark or text).
  3. Where it appears in Study-Smart (page, screenshot, and version or date).
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the rights owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that your complaint is accurate and that you are the rights owner or authorised to act for them.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.

What we do

  • We aim to acknowledge complaints within [[REVIEW: e.g. 5 business days]].
  • We review the complaint and may ask for more details.
  • If it is valid, we remove or change the material in the next update and tell you.
  • If we disagree, we explain why. You can reply with more information.
  • Universities or colleges that do not want their name in the app's presets can ask us to remove it, whether or not a legal claim is involved.
