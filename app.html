Before using AI features

AI features send what you type, or the cleaned image of a page you scan, directly from this browser to Google's Gemini API using your own API key. Study-Smart does not receive it.

Google handles it under the Gemini API terms you accepted. On Google's free tier, Google may use prompts and responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Don't send personal or confidential information, or material you don't have the right to use.

AI answers and flashcards can be wrong. Nothing is saved until you review it.

You can turn this off any time in Settings & Privacy. Privacy Notice
