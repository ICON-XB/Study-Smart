# Global University Architecture

Study-Smart Vision is designed for students worldwide. While it originated in Namibia (and maintains strong built-in presets for NUST, UNAM, and IUM), its architecture is data-driven and agnostic.

## Presets
We maintain a list of prominent global universities in data/universities.js as convenience presets to speed up the onboarding process for students from Africa, Europe, Asia, North America, South America, and Oceania.

**Important:** These presets do NOT imply official partnership, endorsement, or integration with Study-Smart unless explicitly stated. They are purely for student convenience.

## Add My University
If a student's institution isn't listed, they can manually enter their details ("Add My University"). The Agentic Vision system (Smart Scan) can also automatically detect an unknown university from a course syllabus and propose adding it to the student's local profile, requiring explicit confirmation. User-created institutions are stored securely in local data and are not broadcast to a global database.
