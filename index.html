Keep studying when the connection doesn't.

Study-Smart is Offline-First. Saved modules, flashcards, spaced repetition, your study schedule, and Pomodoro timers remain available even when connectivity isn't.

Your academic material belongs to you.

Study-Smart preprocesses documents locally on your device. Cloud processing requires your consent, and temporary images are auto-deleted. Your study data is encrypted locally using AES-GCM.
