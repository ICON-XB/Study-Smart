Build your semester from the documents you already have.

Instead of typing everything in, add photos of your course materials. With your own Google Gemini API key, Study-Smart suggests modules and assessment dates. You check and correct everything before it is saved.

OpenCV inside

Your camera becomes a study tool.

Study-Smart Vision uses OpenCV on your device to check and clean up each photo. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on AI features with your own key.

  • ✓ Document boundary detection
  • ✓ Perspective correction (Deskew)
  • ✓ Blur analysis (Laplacian Variance)
  • ✓ Lighting assessment
  • ✓ Bandwidth optimization (Crop & Compress)

Keep studying when the connection doesn't.

Study-Smart is Offline-First. Saved modules, flashcards, spaced repetition, your study schedule, and Pomodoro timers remain available even when connectivity isn't.

Your academic material belongs to you.

Your study data stays in your browser, encrypted with AES-GCM using a key made from your PIN. Photos are processed on your device. AI features are optional: only if you add your own Gemini key and agree does anything go to Google. There are no accounts, ads or trackers. Read the Privacy Notice.
